## 1. Core Pricing Model

### Settings (Nurseries)
- **Pilot Pricing:** £99/month subscription (60% discount, locked for 12 months)
- **Full Pricing:** £249/month subscription
- **Hourly Rate:** £22/hour (includes all costs)
- **Emergency Bonus:** £10 flat fee (if backup carer needed)
- **Payment Terms:** Monthly billing (subscription + hourly fees from previous month)

### Carers
- **Hourly Rate:** £15.50/hour (all levels, ongoing)
- **Payment Terms:** Net-30 from invoice submission
- **Employment Status:** Self-employed contractors
- **SMS Charges:** £0.10 per message PAYG (deducted from payment, optional)
- **Travel Compensation:** £5 if late no-show loses shift

### Platform Economics
- **Gross Margin:** £6.50/hour (29.5%)
- **Subscription Revenue:** £990/month (10 pilot) or £2,490/month (10 full price)
- **SMS Profit:** ~£12/month net (from 40% opt-in rate)

## 4. Expansion Strategy: Supply-First Model

### The Principle
**ALWAYS recruit carers BEFORE signing settings in each new borough**

### Why It Works
1. **No chicken-and-egg:** Settings see "8 verified carers ready in your area"
2. **Capital efficient:** Only recruit where expanding
3. **De-risked:** If can't recruit carers, don't launch in that borough
4. **Fast time-to-revenue:** Carers ready → sign settings → revenue in 2 weeks
5. **Quality first:** Verify carers thoroughly before they're needed

### Expansion Sequence (Per Borough)
```
Week 1: Research target area
- Download Ofsted data
- Filter 20-50 child nurseries
- Check demographics
- GO/NO-GO decision

Week 2-3: Recruit Carers FIRST
- Facebook ads: "Nursery work in [Borough] - £15.50/hr"
- Indeed posts: "[Borough] - Flexible Childcare"
- Target: 6-8 verified carers
- HOLD on settings until supply secured

Week 4: Verify Supply
- All carers verified (DBS, quals, references)
- Test response times
- Geographic coverage check
- 80%+ pass = Green light

Week 5+: Launch to Settings
- Email 20 target nurseries
- Pitch: "We have 8 verified carers ready in [Borough]"
- Target: Sign 3-5 settings
- Monitor fill rate (should be >85%)
```

### Carer Acquisition Cost
- Budget per borough: £200
- Target carers: 6-8
- CAC: £25-33/carer
- Channels: Facebook ads (£100) + Indeed (£100)

## 10. Risk Mitigation

### Market Risks
- **Can't recruit carers:** Don't launch in that borough
- **Low fill rate (<70%):** Add more carers or exit borough
- **High churn (>30%):** Wrong market fit, pivot or exit

### Operational Risks
- **No-show:** Secondary carer backup system + £10 bonus
- **Quality issues:** Strike system, ratings, removal process
- **Safeguarding incident:** £2M Professional Indemnity insurance

### Financial Risks
- **Cash flow:** Upfront charging + Net-30 carer payments = positive working capital
- **Low usage:** Breakeven at just 8.4 hrs/setting (very safe)
- **Price sensitivity:** 45-55% cheaper than agencies (strong value)
