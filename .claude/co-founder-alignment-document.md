## Part 3: Pricing & Business Model 💰

### ✅ **Decisions Already Made:**

**Pricing Structure:**
- Settings pay: £22/hour + £99-249/month subscription
- Carers earn: £15.50/hour
- Platform margin: £6.50/hour (29.5%)

**Why these numbers were chosen:**
- £22/hour: 34-61% cheaper than traditional agencies (£30-40/hr)
- £15.50/hour: Competitive for carers (above minimum wage, fair market rate)
- Subscription: Captures committed users, creates recurring revenue
