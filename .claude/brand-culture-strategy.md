# Carer Supply Management - Quality & Retention Strategy

**Purpose:** Attract, retain, and motivate professional carers who deliver exceptional service to our customers (settings)  
**Date Created:** 15 February 2026  
**Status:** Living Document

---

## 🎯 The Goal (REVISED)

**Critical Understanding:**
- **Settings are the CUSTOMERS** (they pay £4-6K/year)
- **Carers are the SUPPLY** (they deliver the service settings buy)
- Platform exists to serve settings FIRST

**Therefore, we need carers who:**
1. ✅ Show up reliably (no-show rate <3%)
2. ✅ Deliver professional, high-quality childcare
3. ✅ Generate 4.5+ star ratings from settings
4. ✅ Stay on platform long enough to build consistency (>6 months)
5. ✅ Recommend other quality carers (expand supply)
6. References for staff must include setting reference with work email from setting domain name as part of verification
7. qualifying questions: 
	- DBS check on update service
	- safeguarding training
	- paediatric first aid
	- Level of childcare qualification
	- staff MUST be on verification update service

**NOT the goal:**
- ❌ Make carers "love" the platform (nice-to-have, not essential)
- ❌ Build carer loyalty over customer loyalty
- ❌ Prioritize carer happiness over setting satisfaction

**The Right Balance:**
Settings must LOVE us → Carers must be SATISFIED enough to deliver quality service

## 💡 Business Model Clarity

### The Hierarchy:

**1. SETTINGS (The Customers) - Must LOVE the platform**
- Pay £4,000-6,000/year each
- Source of all revenue
- Decide if platform succeeds or fails
- Focus: Obsessive customer satisfaction

**2. CARERS (The Supply) - Must be PROFESSIONAL and SATISFIED**
- Paid £15.50/hour (fair market rate)
- Self-employed contractors
- Deliver the service settings buy
- Focus: Quality, reliability, retention

**3. PLATFORM (The Facilitator)**
- Matches supply to demand
- Ensures quality standards
- Manages risk and disputes
- Focus: Both-sided marketplace health

### What This Means:

**When Settings and Carers Conflict:**
- Settings win (they're the customer)
- Example: Setting says carer was unprofessional → Carer removed
- BUT: Fair investigation process (not arbitrary)

**Investment Priority:**
- 70% of effort → Making settings love us
- 30% of effort → Keeping carers professional and available

**Success Metrics:**
- Primary: Setting retention, NPS, referrals
- Secondary: Carer quality scores, retention
- Platform wins when settings get reliable, quality carers

## Part 1: Understanding What Carers Hate About Traditional Agencies

### Common Pain Points (Validated Through Research):

**1. Feeling Like a Number**
- "They only call when they need me"
- "No one knows my name"
- "I'm just a body to fill a gap"
- Assigned random ID numbers
- Generic mass texts

**2. Lack of Respect**
- Last-minute calls (even at 6 AM)
- No apology when plans change
- Expected to always say yes
- Treated as lower status than permanent staff
- "Beggars can't be choosers" attitude

**3. Payment Issues**
- Unclear rates (varies by setting)
- Late payments (30+ days)
- Disputes about hours worked
- Hidden deductions
- No transparency

**4. No Career Development**
- Dead-end work
- No training opportunities
- No progression path
- Skills not recognized
- Experience doesn't matter

**5. Isolation**
- Work alone at different settings
- Never meet other agency workers
- No community or connection
- No one to ask for advice
- Feel like outsider everywhere

**6. Unpredictability**
- Don't know weekly income
- Can't plan personal life
- Feast or famine work
- No control over schedule
- Constant uncertainty

### 🌟 **Pillar 5: Transparency & Trust**

**Philosophy:** "No hidden agendas, no surprises, complete honesty"

**Radical Transparency:**

**About Money:**
- Show exactly how pricing works
  - "Settings pay £22/hour"
  - "You receive £15.50/hour"
  - "£6.50 covers platform costs, insurance, tech, support"
- Share financial health (when appropriate)
  - "We hit £10K MRR! Thank you for making this possible"
- Explain price changes upfront
  - "We're increasing to £16/hour because [clear reason]"

**About Decisions:**
- Explain why policies exist
  - "We have a strike system to protect settings and other carers"
  - "GPS check-in helps prevent fraud and protects you in disputes"
- Ask for input before major changes
  - "We're considering [X]. What do you think?"
  - Survey before implementing

**About Problems:**
- Admit mistakes publicly
  - "We messed up. Here's what happened and how we're fixing it"
- Share challenges honestly
  - "We're struggling to recruit in [borough]. Ideas?"
- Show what you're working on
  - Public roadmap of features
  - "What we're building next"

**About Data:**
- Show aggregated stats
  - "Average earnings this month: £847"
  - "Most popular borough: Southwark"
  - "Highest rated carers earn 23% more"
- Personal stats
  - "You've worked with 8 different settings"
  - "You're in top 20% of rated carers"

### 🌟 **Pillar 6: Fairness & Protection**

**Philosophy:** "We have your back, always"

**How We Protect Carers:**

**Payment Protection:**
- ✅ Guaranteed payment even if setting cancels (<24 hrs)
- ✅ Hold setting's payment until dispute resolved
- ✅ No "not delivered" SMS charges
- ✅ Transparent invoice (see every penny)
- ✅ Emergency fund for genuine hardship

**Safety Protection:**
- ✅ Right to refuse unsafe situations (no strike)
- ✅ 24/7 support line for emergencies
- ✅ Incident reporting with follow-up
- ✅ Block settings that mistreat carers
- ✅ Support in disputes

**Reputation Protection:**
- ✅ Settings can't leave anonymous bad reviews
- ✅ You can respond to any feedback
- ✅ Mediation for disputes
- ✅ Remove unfair reviews
- ✅ Private appeals process

**Fair Treatment:**
- ✅ Strike system is progressive (second chances)
- ✅ Appeals allowed and considered
- ✅ Context matters (life happens)
- ✅ Consistent rules for everyone
- ✅ Clear, written policies (no surprises)

## Part 3: Making Carers Brand Ambassadors

### 🎯 **Referral Program That Actually Works**

**Why Traditional Referral Programs Fail:**
- Payouts too low (£10-25)
- Paid only after referee completes X shifts (too long)
- Complicated rules
- Don't track progress
- Feel transactional

**Our Approach:**

**"Grow Together" Referral Program:**

```
TIER 1: Refer a Friend
You earn: £50
Friend earns: £50
Unlocked: After friend's 5th shift
Both paid within 7 days

TIER 2: Build Your Crew (3+ referrals)
You earn: £75 per referral (ongoing)
You unlock: "Recruiter" badge
Bonus: £100 when 3rd referral completes 5 shifts

TIER 3: Borough Ambassador (10+ referrals)
You earn: £100 per referral
You become: Borough Lead (paid role option)
Bonus: Trip to [destination] for you + friend

LIFETIME EARNINGS TRACKER:
"You've earned £450 in referrals! 
2 more referrals to unlock Borough Ambassador"
```

**Make It Easy:**
- Personal referral link (tracks automatically)
- Pre-written message templates
- Share on WhatsApp/Facebook with one click
- Show progress in real-time
- Celebrate publicly when paid

## Part 5: Measuring Success

### 📊 **Brand Health Metrics**

**Monthly Tracking:**

**Referral Rate:**
- Target: 30% of carers refer at least one person/year
- Measure: Referrals per 100 active carers

**Net Promoter Score (NPS):**
- Question: "How likely are you to recommend us to other carers?"
- Target: NPS > 50 (excellent)
- Measure: Monthly survey

**Engagement Rate:**
- Community group participation
- Newsletter open rate (target: >40%)
- Event attendance
- Content creation participation

**Retention Rate:**
- 30-day retention: >80%
- 90-day retention: >60%
- 1-year retention: >40%

**Pride Indicators:**
- LinkedIn badge adoption: >50%
- Social media mentions
- Brand item usage (photos shared)
- Unsolicited testimonials

---

### 💬 **Feedback Loops**

**Quarterly Carer Survey:**
1. How valued do you feel? (1-10)
2. How likely to recommend? (NPS)
3. What do you love most?
4. What frustrates you?
5. What would make you leave?
6. What should we build next?

**Exit Interviews:**
- When carer stops working
- Understand why
- Learn and improve

- Monthly quick survey (3 questions)
- Temperature check
- Catch issues early
